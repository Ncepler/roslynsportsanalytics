Source: Roslyn High School's Hudl Fan page (fan.hudl.com, org 18883). Checked signed out, then re-checked signed in as Noah Cepler (read-only): the 11 "no video" teams looked identical in both views. Every one of the 36 teams was checked: the "no video" teams were each opened on their own page, and the school-wide Livestreams and Highlights lists were read in full.
